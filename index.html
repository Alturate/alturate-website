Why AI stalls

The same four blockers show up again and again.

Dirty ERP data

Duplicate masters, missing descriptions, and inconsistent records make Copilot outputs feel generic or unsafe to trust.

No role-based setup

Finance, supply chain, sales, and leadership each need different workflows. Generic setup does not drive adoption.

No visible ROI

If leadership cannot see time saved or usage by team, Copilot renewals become hard to justify.

Long deployment cycles

Traditional projects move too slowly for SMBs. By the time AI goes live, momentum is already gone.
