How We Started

The Gap Nobody
Was Filling

After years working inside Microsoft Dynamics implementations, we kept seeing the same painful pattern. Companies would invest in Dynamics 365, pay for Copilot licenses, and then watch their teams revert to spreadsheets within 90 days.

The problem wasn't the technology. Microsoft's AI is genuinely powerful. The problem was the gap between buying a license and actually making it work — clean data, configured agents, measured ROI.

Big system integrators charged $200K and took 12 months. Microsoft's own documentation assumed deep technical expertise. SMBs were left completely alone.

Alturate was founded to close that gap. We built the pre-configured AI activation layer that every Dynamics customer needs but nobody was selling — and we made it deployable in 14 days, not 14 months.
